ROLE

Job Description

A NLP Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A NLP Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A NLP Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Clean and preprocess text.
  • Build NLP models.
  • Train text classification systems.
  • Work with embeddings and transformers.
  • Integrate language models.
  • Deploy and monitor NLP solutions.
