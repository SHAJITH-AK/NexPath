Cloud Engineer

Career guide covering skills, responsibilities, salary and companies hiring cloud engineers.

ROLE

Job Description

A Cloud Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Cloud Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Cloud Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Design cloud infrastructure.
  • Deploy and manage cloud applications.
  • Configure networking and access controls.
  • Automate infrastructure provisioning.
  • Monitor cloud resources.
  • Optimize reliability and cloud costs.
