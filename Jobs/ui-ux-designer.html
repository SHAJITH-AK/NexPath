UI/UX Designer

Career guide covering skills, responsibilities, salary and companies hiring ui/ux designers.

ROLE

Job Description

A UI/UX Designer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A UI/UX Designer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A UI/UX Designer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Research user needs.
  • Create user flows and wireframes.
  • Design interfaces and prototypes.
  • Build design systems.
  • Test designs and iterate.
  • Collaborate with developers and product teams.
