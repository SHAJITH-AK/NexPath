Cyber Security Engineer

Career guide covering skills, responsibilities, salary and companies hiring cyber security engineers.

ROLE

Job Description

A Cyber Security Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Cyber Security Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Cyber Security Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Identify and assess security risks.
  • Monitor systems for suspicious activity.
  • Implement security controls.
  • Investigate and respond to incidents.
  • Test systems for vulnerabilities.
  • Maintain security documentation.
