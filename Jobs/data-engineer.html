Data Engineer

Career guide covering skills, responsibilities, salary and companies hiring data engineers.

ROLE

Job Description

A Data Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Data Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Data Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Build reliable data pipelines.
  • Collect and transform data.
  • Design databases and warehouses.
  • Process large-scale datasets.
  • Monitor data pipeline quality.
  • Support analytics and ML teams.
