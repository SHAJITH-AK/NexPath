Computer Vision Engineer

Career guide covering skills, responsibilities, salary and companies hiring computer vision engineers.

ROLE

Job Description

A Computer Vision Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Computer Vision Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Computer Vision Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Process image and video datasets.
  • Develop computer vision models.
  • Train detection and recognition systems.
  • Improve model performance.
  • Deploy vision models.
  • Monitor computer vision systems.
