ROLE

Job Description

A DevOps Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A DevOps Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A DevOps Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines.
  • Automate application deployments.
  • Manage containers and cloud infrastructure.
  • Monitor applications and infrastructure.
  • Improve system reliability.
  • Collaborate with development and operations teams.
