Database Engineer

Career guide covering skills, responsibilities, salary and companies hiring database engineers.

ROLE

Job Description

A Database Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Database Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Database Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Design database schemas.
  • Write and optimize SQL queries.
  • Manage database performance.
  • Implement backup and recovery.
  • Control database access.
  • Monitor production databases.
