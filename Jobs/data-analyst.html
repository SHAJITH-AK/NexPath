ROLE

Job Description

A Data Analyst applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Data Analyst may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Data Analyst develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Collect and clean business data.
  • Write queries to analyze data.
  • Identify trends and patterns.
  • Build dashboards and reports.
  • Prepare insights for stakeholders.
  • Communicate data-driven recommendations.
