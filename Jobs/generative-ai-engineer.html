ROLE

Job Description

A Generative AI Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Generative AI Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Generative AI Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Design Generative AI applications.
  • Integrate AI models through APIs.
  • Build retrieval-augmented generation systems.
  • Create prompts and AI workflows.
  • Evaluate model quality and reliability.
  • Deploy and monitor AI applications.
