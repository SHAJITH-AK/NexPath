Network Engineer

Career guide covering skills, responsibilities, salary and companies hiring network engineers.

ROLE

Job Description

A Network Engineer applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Network Engineer may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Network Engineer develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Configure network infrastructure.
  • Manage routers, switches and firewalls.
  • Troubleshoot connectivity issues.
  • Monitor network health.
  • Implement network security controls.
  • Automate network operations.
