Career guide covering skills, responsibilities, salary and companies hiring data scientists.

ROLE

Job Description

A Data Scientist applies technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to build, manage or improve professional technology solutions.

Professionals in this role use specialized tools, programming concepts and engineering practices to solve real-world business and technical problems.

A Data Scientist may work across development, testing, deployment, optimization and maintenance depending on the organization and specialization.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Duties and Responsibilities

A Data Scientist develops reliable solutions according to technical and business requirements.

  • Collect, clean and analyze datasets.
  • Identify patterns and trends.
  • Build and evaluate predictive models.
  • Create visualizations and reports.
  • Communicate data-driven insights.
  • Deploy and monitor models.
